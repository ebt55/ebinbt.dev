summary: "Hidden principals can be recovered from heavily poisoned training data, given one clean corpus from the same generator, with a per-draw median of 26% top-1 among 47 candidates (range 10% to 44%). At the poison densities real attacks use, the signal falls to near chance."
---

An existence proof and a boundary. Off-the-shelf embedders beat a 2.1% chance rate by a wide margin with no knowledge of the attacker's prompt, then fall to 1.2 to 1.8 times chance at 3% poison density, and the method ranks without detecting.
